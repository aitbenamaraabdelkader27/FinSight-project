# US GAAP Overview

## Generally Accepted Accounting Principles

US GAAP is the accounting standard used in the United States. It's developed by the FASB (Financial Accounting Standards Board).

## Key Differences: US GAAP vs IFRS

| Area | US GAAP | IFRS |
|------|---------|------|
| **Inventory** | LIFO allowed | LIFO prohibited |
| **Development Costs** | Usually expensed | Capitalize if criteria met |
| **Lease Classification** | Different thresholds | More principles-based |
| **Revaluation Model** | Not allowed | Allowed for PPE |
| **Impairment Reversals** | Not allowed | Allowed |
| **Financial Statement Order** | Assets → Liabilities → Equity | Equity → Liabilities → Assets |

## Major Accounting Standards (ASC Topics)

### ASC 606 - Revenue Recognition
Similar to IFRS 15:
1. Identify contract
2. Identify performance obligations
3. Determine transaction price
4. Allocate to performance obligations
5. Recognize revenue

### ASC 842 - Leases
Replaced ASC 840 (2019 for public, 2021 for private)
- Lessees: All leases on balance sheet
- Similar to IFRS 16

### ASC 326 - Current Expected Credit Losses (CECL)
- Replaced incurred loss model
- Expected lifetime losses
- Forward-looking information

### ASC 740 - Income Taxes
- Deferred tax accounting
- Valuation allowances
- Uncertain tax positions (FIN 48)

## Key Principles

### Historical Cost
Assets recorded at purchase price

### Matching Principle
Expenses matched with revenues

### Revenue Recognition
- Earned and realized/realizable
- PEC (persuasive evidence, delivery, determinable)

### Full Disclosure
All material facts in financial statements

## Financial Statement Order (US GAAP)

1. Balance Sheet (Statement of Financial Position)
2. Income Statement (Statement of Operations)
3. Statement of Comprehensive Income
4. Statement of Changes in Equity
5. Statement of Cash Flows
6. Notes to Financial Statements

## Common Size Presentations

### Balance Sheet
- Assets = Liabilities + Equity
- Current assets listed first
- Current liabilities listed first

### Income Statement
- Multiple formats allowed
- Function or nature classification
- Includes discontinued operations
