# IFRS 16 - Leases

## Overview

IFRS 16 specifies how to recognise, measure, present and disclose leases. The standard provides a single lessee accounting model, requiring lessees to recognise assets and liabilities for all leases unless the lease term is 12 months or less or the underlying asset has a low value.

## Key Changes from IAS 17

### For Lessees
- **Operating leases** → Now recognized on balance sheet
- **Finance leases** → Similar treatment to IAS 17

### For Lessors
- Largely unchanged from IAS 17
- Continued distinction between operating and finance leases

## Recognition Criteria

A contract is, or contains, a lease if it conveys the right to control the use of an identified asset for a period of time in exchange for consideration.

### Identified Asset
- Explicitly specified in the contract
- Physically distinct
- Supplier does not have substantive right to substitute

### Control
- Right to obtain substantially all economic benefits
- Right to direct the use of the identified asset

## Measurement

### Lease Liability
Present value of:
- Fixed payments (including in-substance fixed)
- Variable lease payments linked to index/rate
- Amounts expected payable under residual value guarantees
- Exercise price for purchase options (if reasonably certain)
- Penalties for termination (if lease term reflects exercise)

### Right-of-Use Asset
Initial measurement:
- Lease liability
- Plus: any lease payments made before commencement
- Plus: initial direct costs
- Less: any lease incentives received

Subsequent measurement:
- Cost model: depreciation + interest
- Revaluation model (if elected)

## Lease Term

Determined as the non-cancellable period:
- Plus periods covered by extension option (if reasonably certain)
- Plus periods covered by termination option (if reasonably certain)

## Disclosure Requirements

### Quantitative
- Reconciliation of lease commitments
- Maturity analysis of lease liabilities
- Total cash outflow for leases
- Interest expense on lease liabilities

### Qualitative
- Nature of leases (significant assumptions)
- Restrictions imposed by leases
- Sale and leaseback transactions

## Effective Date

- **Annual periods** beginning on or after 1 January 2019
- Earlier application permitted (if IFRS 15 also applied)

## Practical Exemptions

1. **Short-term leases** (≤ 12 months)
2. **Low-value assets** (≤ $5,000)
   - Includes small items of furniture, tablets, etc.
   - Not exemption for leased assets underlying sub-leases
