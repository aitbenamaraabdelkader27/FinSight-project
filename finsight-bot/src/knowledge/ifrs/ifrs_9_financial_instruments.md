# IFRS 9 - Financial Instruments

## Overview

IFRS 9 Financial Instruments replaces IAS 39 and provides a single approach to:
1. Classification and measurement
2. Impairment methodology
3. Hedge accounting

## Classification & Measurement

### Financial Assets (3 categories)

| Category | Measurement | Business Model |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Amortized Cost** | SPPI cash flows + hold to collect | Hold |
| **FVOCI** | Fair value + recycling | Hold + Sell |
| **FVTPL** | Fair value | Other / Trading |

### SPPI Test (Solely Payments of Principal and Interest)
- Must pass for amortized cost or FVOCI
- Tests if cash flows are purely principal + interest

### Financial Liabilities
- Generally at amortized cost
- FVTPL for trading liabilities or designated

## Impairment (Expected Credit Loss - ECL)

### 3-Stage Model

| Stage | Description | ECL Measurement |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Stage 1** | No significant increase in credit risk | 12-month ECL |
| **Stage 2** | Significant increase in credit risk | Lifetime ECL |
| **Stage 3** | Credit-impaired | Lifetime ECL |

### Simplified Approach
- Trade receivables: Always lifetime ECL
- Contract assets: Lifetime ECL

## Hedge Accounting

### Types of Hedges
- **Fair value hedge**: Hedge of exposure to changes in fair value
- **Cash flow hedge**: Hedge of exposure to variability in cash flows
- **Hedge of net investment in foreign operation**

### Effectiveness Requirements
- Prospective effectiveness: 80-125% range
- Documentation required at inception

## Key Changes from IAS 39

| Area | IAS 39 | IFRS 9 |
|------|--------|--------|
| Classification | 4 categories | 3 categories |
| Impairment | Incurred loss | Expected loss |
| Hedge effectiveness | 80-125% | Qualitative assessment |
| Embedded derivatives | Complex rules | Simplified |

## Effective Date

- **Annual periods** beginning on or after 1 January 2018
- Earlier application permitted
