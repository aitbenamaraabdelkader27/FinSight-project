# IAS 16 - Property, Plant and Equipment

## Overview

 IAS 16 provides guidance on accounting for property, plant and equipment (PPE), including:
- Recognition
- Measurement (initial and subsequent)
- Depreciation
- Derecognition

## Recognition

An item is recognized as PPE when:
1. **Probable** future economic benefits will flow to entity
2. **Cost** can be measured reliably

## Initial Measurement

Cost includes:
- Purchase price (excluding VAT recoverable)
- Import duties
- Non-refundable taxes
- Direct costs of bringing asset to location
- Estimated costs of dismantling/removal

### Examples of Costs Included
- Site preparation
- Delivery and handling
- Installation and testing
- Professional fees

### Costs NOT Included
- Costs of opening new facility
- Training costs
- Administrative overhead

## Subsequent Measurement

### Cost Model
- Asset carried at cost
- Less accumulated depreciation
- Less accumulated impairment losses

### Revaluation Model (Optional)
- Fair value at revaluation date
- Revaluations must be regular (3-5 years)
- All assets in same class revalued together
- Surplus to OCI (revaluation reserve)
- Deficit to profit or loss

## Depreciation

### Depreciable Amount
Cost - Residual value

### Methods
| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Straight-line** | Equal expense each year |
| **Diminishing balance** | Higher expense early |
| **Units of production** | Based on usage |

### Review
- Depreciation method reviewed annually
- Residual value reviewed annually
- Changes accounted for prospectively

## Derecognition

Asset is derecognized when:
- Disposed, OR
- No future economic benefits expected

### Gain/Loss on Disposal
= Net disposal proceeds - Carrying amount
= Recognized in profit or loss

## Disclosure Requirements

For each class of PPE:
- Measurement bases
- Depreciation methods
- Useful lives or rates
- Gross carrying amount
- Accumulated depreciation
- Reconciliation of carrying amount
