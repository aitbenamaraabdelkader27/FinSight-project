# IFRS 15 - Revenue from Contracts with Customers

## Overview

IFRS 15 establishes a single comprehensive model for revenue recognition and measurement. It applies to all revenue arising from contracts with customers, except for contracts that are within the scope of other standards (e.g., IFRS 16, IFRS 9, IAS 17).

## The 5-Step Model

### Step 1: Identify the Contract
A contract is an agreement between two or more parties that creates enforceable rights and obligations. Criteria:
- Contract has commercial substance
- Payment terms are identifiable
- Probability of collection

### Step 2: Identify Performance Obligations
A performance obligation is a promise to transfer to the customer:
- A distinct good or service
- A series of distinct goods or services

### Step 3: Determine the Transaction Price
Consider:
- Fixed consideration
- Variable consideration (constraints apply)
- Time value of money (if significant)
- Non-cash consideration
- Consideration payable to customer

### Step 4: Allocate Transaction Price
Allocate to performance obligations based on relative standalone selling prices:
- Observable price if available
- Estimate if not directly observable

### Step 5: Recognize Revenue
Revenue is recognized when (or as) performance obligations are satisfied:
- Point in time, OR
- Over time

## Key Concepts

### Control
Revenue is recognized when control of goods/services transfers to customer. Indicators:
- Customer has present right to payment
- Customer has legal title
- Physical possession transferred
- Significant risks/benefits transferred
- Customer has accepted the asset

### Variable Consideration
Estimated using either:
- **Expected value**: Most likely amount (for many outcomes)
- **Most likely amount**: Single most likely outcome (for binary outcomes)

Constrained until highly probable of reversal.

### Constraints on Estimates
Revenue can only be included if a significant reversal is not probable.

## Principal vs Agent

- **Principal**: Controls goods/services before transfer → Revenue gross
- **Agent**: Arranges for another to provide → Revenue net (fee)

## Contract Costs

### Incremental Costs of Obtaining Contract
- Capitalize if expected to be recovered
- Amortize over period of benefit

### Costs to Fulfill Contract
- Capitalize if related to performance obligation
- Otherwise, expense as incurred

## Disclosure Requirements

### Qualitative
- Nature, timing, amount of revenue
- Performance obligations
- Transaction price allocation
- Significant judgments

### Quantitative
- Revenue by major category
- Contract balances (assets, liabilities)
- Performance obligations

## Effective Date

- **Annual periods** beginning on or after 1 January 2018
- Earlier application permitted
