# Year-End Audit Checklist

## Pre-Year-End Planning

### 4-6 Weeks Before Year-End
- [ ] Hold planning meeting with audit team
- [ ] Review prior year audit adjustments
- [ ] Update audit timeline and milestones
- [ ] Identify new accounting standards impact
- [ ] Obtain understanding of new transactions
- [ ] Review group instructions and deadlines

### 2-4 Weeks Before Year-End
- [ ] Send cutoff letters to banks
- [ ] Send confirmations to customers/suppliers
- [ ] Complete physical inventory observations
- [ ] Review contingent liabilities
- [ ] Update tax calculations
- [ ] Review related party transactions

## Year-End Procedures

### Assets

#### Cash and Bank
- [ ] Bank reconciliations complete
- [ ] Bank confirmations received
- [ ] Review for unpresented checks
- [ ] Review for deposits in transit
- [ ] Verify foreign currency translations

#### Accounts Receivable
- [ ] Age analysis reviewed
- [ ] Allowance for doubtful accounts assessed
- [ ] Review credit notes after year-end
- [ ] Confirm significant balances
- [ ] Review billing cutoff

#### Inventory
- [ ] Physical inventory count completed
- [ ] Inventory valuation reviewed
- [ ] Slow-moving inventory identified
- [ ] Review bill of materials
- [ ] Verify consignment inventory

#### Fixed Assets
- [ ] Asset register reconciled
- [ ] Depreciation calculation reviewed
- [ ] Asset disposals identified
- [ ] Capitalization policies followed
- [ ] Impairment review completed

### Liabilities

#### Accounts Payable
- [ ] Age analysis reviewed
- [ ] Accruals for un-invoiced goods/services
- [ ] Review cutoff procedures
- [ ] Confirm significant balances

#### Accruals
- [ ] Accrued expenses listed and reviewed
- [ ] Holiday pay calculation
- [ ] Bonus accruals
- [ ] Legal provisions
- [ ] Environmental provisions

#### Debt
- [ ] Debt confirmations received
- [ ] Covenant calculations reviewed
- [ ] Interest accruals calculated

### Equity

- [ ] Share capital movements reviewed
- [ ] Dividend declarations verified
- [ ] Retained earnings reconciliation
- [ ] Other comprehensive income reviewed

## Revenue and Expenses

### Revenue
- [ ] Revenue recognition policy applied
- [ ] Cutoff procedures followed
- [ ] Bill and hold transactions reviewed
- [ ] Revenue deducting reviewed
- [ ] Contract asset/liability analysis

### Expenses
- [ ] Prepayments reviewed
- [ ] Accruals complete
- [ ] Intercompany eliminations
- [ ] Expense allocation reviewed

## Financial Reporting

### IFRS/GAAP Compliance
- [ ] All standards applied correctly
- [ ] Disclosures complete
- [ ] Prior year comparatives reviewed
- [ ] Events after reporting date considered

### Management Representations
- [ ] Representation letter drafted
- [ ] Completeness of information verified

## Audit Finalization

- [ ] Audit adjustments posted
- [ ] Draft financial statements prepared
- [ ] Audit committee presentation ready
- [ ] Regulatory filing prepared
- [ ] File documentation complete
