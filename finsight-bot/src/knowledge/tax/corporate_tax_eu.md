## Key Deductions

### R&D Tax Credits
| Country | Credit Rate |
|---------|-------------|
| France | 30% (first €100M) |
| UK | 33% (RDEC) |
| Ireland | 25% (RDEC) |
| Netherlands | 32% (WBSO) |

### Interest Limitation (ATAD)
- Max deductible interest: 30% of EBITDA
- Threshold: €1M deductible always
- Group interest rules apply

### Loss Carry-forward
- Generally unlimited in EU
- May have time limits in some countries
- Anti-avoidance rules apply

## Transfer Pricing

### Documentation (EU)
- **Master File**: Group overview
- **Local File**: Specific transactions
- **CbCR**: Country-by-country reporting (>€750M revenue)

### Safe Harbors
- Low value-adding services: 5% markup
- Loans: Arm's length interest rates
- R&D: Cost plus 15-25%

## Substance Requirements

Countries requiring substance:
- Ireland: Board meetings, key decisions
- Netherlands: Office, employees, decision-making
- Luxembourg: Real economic activity

## Anti-Avoidance Rules (ATAD)

1. **CFC Rules**: Parent taxed on subsidiary's passive income
2. **Exit Taxation**: Tax on unrealized gains on migration
3. **Interest Limitation**: 30% EBITDA cap
4. **GAAR**: General anti-avoidance rule
5. **Hybrid Mismatch**: Deduction/no-inclusion rules
